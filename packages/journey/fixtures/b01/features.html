Everything you need to stay organized

Four capabilities, no sprawling admin console. Each one earns its place.

Fast capture

A new note is one keystroke away, from anywhere in the app. Titles are optional; everything is searchable the moment it exists.

  • Instant note creation with a single shortcut
  • Optional titles — first line becomes the title when you want one
  • Everything indexed as you type

Smart linking

Notes mention each other with plain links. Backlinks assemble themselves, and the graph view stays readable even at a thousand notes.

  • Plain-text mentions that resolve to notes
  • Automatic backlinks on every note
  • A graph you can actually read

Calm collaboration

Share a space, mention a teammate, move on. Nimbus shows you what changed since you last looked — not an infinite activity feed.

  • Spaces with simple invite links
  • Mentions that arrive as a short daily digest
  • Per-note read markers

Offline first

The local copy is the source of truth. Sync happens in the background and reconciles quietly when you reconnect.

  • Full read and write access offline
  • Conflict-free merges for plain notes
  • Sync status you can see at a glance
